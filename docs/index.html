你好，我是谢之非，祖籍安徽，在北京长大。目前，我是 LV-Lab 的博士生，导师为颜水成教授和苗春燕教授。在此之前，我本科毕业于北京理工大学，后保研至清华大学，并曾在千问实习，由徐进老师指导。

我的研究方向主要包括以音频为核心的多模态理解和语音智能体。我对研究工作的要求是，它必须具有足够的价值，并秉持这一理念完成了 Mini-Omni 等一系列工作。未来，我将持续探索可实时交互的多模态模型，围绕交互性、鲁棒性、推理能力和主动性等问题展开研究。

除此之外，我热爱交响乐，是一名拥有 16 年经验的乐手。我也非常喜欢动物。我的很多工作都以柴犬作为头像，因为我很怀念一只陪伴我长大的柴犬，它叫瓜瓜。

Hi, I am Zhifei Xie. My family is originally from Anhui, and I grew up in Beijing. I am currently a PhD student at LV-Lab, advised by Prof. Shuineng Yan and Prof. Chunyan Miao. Before that, I received my bachelor's degree from Beijing Institute of Technology, was admitted to Tsinghua University for graduate study, and interned at Qwen under the supervision of Jin Xu.

My research mainly focuses on audio-centric multimodal understanding and speech agents. I believe research should provide sufficient real-world value, and this principle has guided a series of projects including Mini-Omni. Going forward, I will continue exploring real-time interactive multimodal models, with particular interests in interactivity, robustness, reasoning, and proactivity.

Outside research, I love symphonic music and have 16 years of experience as an orchestral musician. I also love animals. Many of my projects use a Shiba Inu as their avatar because I deeply miss Guagua, a Shiba Inu who grew up with me.
