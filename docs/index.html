Biography

I am a Ph.D. student at LV-Lab, Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Shuicheng Yan and Prof. Chunyan Miao. Before that I received my bachelor's degree from Beijing Institute of Technology, was admitted to Tsinghua University for graduate study, and interned at the Qwen Team under the supervision of Dr. Jin Xu.

My research focuses on large audio language models and real-time speech interaction — building models that hear, think and speak at the same time. I led the Mini-Omni series, among the first open-source end-to-end speech-to-speech conversational models, together with Audio-Reasoner, Mini-Omni-Reasoner, Mega-ASR and the Audio Interaction Model. I hold research to one bar: it has to be worth doing. Going forward I keep pushing on always-on multimodal interaction, with a focus on interactivity, robustness, reasoning and proactivity.

Outside the lab I served as founder & CEO of Pask.ai, advise industry teams on speech technology, have played in symphony orchestras for 16 years, and love animals — many of my projects use a Shiba Inu avatar in memory of Guagua, who grew up with me.

Selected Publications * equal contribution  |  full list on Google Scholar

Audio Interaction Model
Zhifei Xie, Zihang Liu, Ze An, Xiaobin Hu, Yue Liao, Ziyang Ma, Dongchao Yang, Mingbao Lin, Deheng Ye, Shuicheng Yan, Chunyan Miao
arXiv, 2026 (Next generation of LALMs)
arXiv / HF paper / Dataset / Code

A unified streaming LALM that listens to sound, environment and instructions in real time and decides, at every moment, whether to stay silent or speak — realised end to end by SoundFlow.

Mega-ASR: Towards In-the-wild² Speech Recognition via Scaling up Real-world Acoustic Simulation
Zhifei Xie, Kaiyu Pang, Haobin Zhang, Deheng Ye, Xiaobin Hu, Shuicheng Yan, Chunyan Miao
arXiv, 2026 (Gold medal, FFASR Leaderboard)
arXiv / Model / Benchmark / Code

Breaking the acoustic robustness bottleneck with Voices-in-the-Wild-2M: 7 classic acoustic phenomena, 54 physically plausible compound scenarios, and WER-gated policy optimisation.

Mini-Omni-Reasoner: Token-Level Thinking-in-Speaking in Large Speech Models
Zhifei Xie, Ziyang Ma, Zihang Liu, Kaiyu Pang, Hongyu Li, Jialin Zhang, Yue Liao, Deheng Ye, Chunyan Miao, Shuicheng Yan
arXiv, 2025
arXiv / Code

Instead of thinking before speaking, the model interleaves silent reasoning tokens with spoken response tokens — fluent, low-latency speech that stays logically grounded.

Audio-Reasoner: Improving Reasoning Capability in Large Audio Language Models
Zhifei Xie, Mingbao Lin, Zihang Liu, Pengcheng Wu, Shuicheng Yan, Chunyan Miao
EMNLP, 2025 (Main Conference)
arXiv / ACL Anthology / Model / Code

The first large audio language model with native in-depth thinking. Trained on CoTA (1.2M reasoning-rich samples), it lifts MMAU-mini by +25.42%.

Mini-Omni2: Towards Open-source GPT-4o with Vision, Speech and Duplex Capabilities
Zhifei Xie, Changqiao Wu
arXiv, 2024
arXiv / Model / Code

An omni-interactive assistant that understands image, audio and text, replies with real-time voice, and can be interrupted mid-sentence — no extra ASR or TTS required.

Mini-Omni: Language Models Can Hear, Talk While Thinking in Streaming
Zhifei Xie, Changqiao Wu
arXiv, 2024 (#1 Paper of the Day, Hugging Face)
arXiv / Model / Demo / Code

The first open-source end-to-end model with direct speech-to-speech capability, using text-instructed speech generation and batch-parallel decoding for real-time conversation.
